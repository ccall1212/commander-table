# Commander Table

[![Version](https://img.shields.io/github/v/tag/ccall1212/commander-table?label=version&sort=semver&color=blue)](CHANGELOG.md) [![License: PolyForm Noncommercial](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-PolyForm%20Noncommercial%201.0.0-blue.svg)](LICENSE)

A free, browser-based Magic: The Gathering Commander table for playing with friends online, in the spirit of Tabletop Simulator. Nothing to install and no accounts.

**Play now:** https://ccall1212.github.io/commander-table/

## What it is

- **Bring your deck:** paste a decklist from Moxfield or Archidekt; card data and images come from Scryfall.
- **Play with friends:** host a game and send the invite link. Friends join from their browser, and a small relay lets the game connect on most networks.
- **A free-form table:** drag cards anywhere, tap, flip, add counters and tokens, attach auras, give a card to another player, and track life, commander damage and turns. Choose a Sidebar or Grid layout, your card back and a table background. iPad and phone play through Touch mode is in **beta**.
- **A sandbox:** like a real table, it doesn't enforce the rules, so players keep each other honest.

## Getting started

1. Open the link above, enter your name, pick or import a deck, and press **Host a game**.
2. Click the room code (top left) to copy an invite link and send it to your friends.
3. They open the link, pick a deck, and press **Join**.
